Explain your reasoning

For a rotation, describe the center and the counterclockwise angle. For a reflection, describe the position and angle of the mirror line. For two moves, use the image after the first move as the starting point for the second move.

Hint for thinking

In each isosceles triangle, focus on the vertex that is the same distance from the other two vertices. Compare the direction it faces before and after the move.
